## Research

**Thesis title:** Exploring Approaches for Improving Large Language Model–Based Translation of Low-Resource Languages

Low-resource languages are languages that have limited digital and computational resources available for Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) tasks, including a shortage of large text corpora, parallel translation datasets, dictionaries, annotated training data, speech datasets, and NLP tools such as tokenizers, parsers, and spell checkers.

This research focuses on **Igbo**, one of Nigeria’s major Indigenous languages. Igbo remains relatively underrepresented in modern NLP resources and digital applications compared to high-resource languages such as English. This research uses English–Igbo translation as a case study because Igbo is a low-resource language with limited NLP resources despite its large speaker population.

The broader objective is to develop a framework that can be adapted to other English–low-resource language translation tasks. If the proposed system succeeds for English–Igbo translation with limited training data, it should transfer to English–other similar low-resource language translation.
